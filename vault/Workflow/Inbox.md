# Inbox

Capture point. Anything that isn't already a Jira ticket or a board row goes here first: a promise made in a meeting, a "look into X", a thing someone asked for in chat, an idea. Don't organise it here; just write it down. `/standup` reads this every morning and proposes where each item goes (Jira ticket, board next action, waiting-for, or drop).

Rule: nothing lives in my head. If it's not in Jira, on the board, in Waiting For, or here, it doesn't exist.
